WHAT IS AI?

Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to the capability of computational systems to perform tasks typically associated with human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making. It is a field of research in computer science that develops and studies methods and software that enable machines to perceive their environment and use learning and intelligence to take actions that maximize their chances of achieving defined goals.[1] Such machines may be called AIs. Large Language Model

A large language model (LLM) is a sophisticated type of machine learning model specifically designed to handle a variety of natural language processing (NLP) tasks, including but not limited to language generation, translation, summarization, and sentiment analysis. LLMs are characterized by their extensive number of parameters, often numbering in the billions, which allows them to capture complex patterns and nuances in human language. These models are typically trained using a method known as self-supervised learning, where they learn to predict the next word in a sentence given the preceding context. This training is conducted on vast and diverse datasets, often comprising text from books, articles, websites, and other written sources. The sheer volume of data helps the models to generalize better and understand a wide range of topics and linguistic structures.
